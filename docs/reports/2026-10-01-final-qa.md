## Findings

1. **Personal data in a committed capture index.** `kb/jurisdictions/kr/clauses/_captures/index.json` holds, as extracted headings of a public Lotte policy page, the name and title of a named executive, a corporate e-mail address and several department phone numbers. The source is public, but the individual's name and address do not belong in the index. Cleaning it changes `captureIndexSha256` in `clauses/index.json` and `manifest.json`, and the index is rebuilt from raw captures that exist only on the original PC, so it was **not** edited here. Decision needed: redact on the original PC (`scripts/build-clauses.ts` after the redaction) or accept as public data.
2. **No clause is vetted and no house-style rule is approved.** The clause-first path and the house-style checks are implemented and tested, but inactive in production until the privacy-domain-expert vets clauses and the user approves rules.
3. **11 clause variables have no interview slot** (document metadata and a few facts). Nine clause files are skipped by the matcher. Pinned in `test/kb-integrity.test.ts`.
4. **C2 covers generic AST checks only.** The rule packs' `check.expr` pseudo-DSL targets a typed section model that the block-based AST does not have, so those rules are covered by the R7 auditor, not evaluated deterministically.
5. **Masking is off by default** (user decision 2026-09-30). Interview and form text reaches the models sanitized and fenced but unmasked. `--masking basic` restores masking before any model call.
6. `packages/cli` is an empty stub; the scripts in `scripts/` are the command line.
