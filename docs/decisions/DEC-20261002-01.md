## Ruling

PM ruling on 2026-10-02 after the council (architect `opus`, privacy-domain-expert `opus`, security-expert `sonnet`, auditor `sonnet`, kb-curator `sonnet`). The user asked for the council and for implementation to follow directly.

1. **Scope (user).** The InfoSec office wants amendment impact and problem alerts for existing policies, in agent (folder) form first and as a URL service later.
2. **Two modes and two tiers.** Mode A (current check) and Mode B (amendment impact). Provisional alerts are automatic, labelled unverified and capped at Medium; confirmed alerts need the privacy-domain-expert's verification and rule-pack update. An absent conditional section is a "confirm" question, never a violation.
3. **Reuse.** C2 runs with a published-policy profile. The fact-based R7 envelope is unchanged; the monitor gets its own auditor input and prompt.
4. **Security.** Ingested text is untrusted and fenced; contacts are masked before LLM calls, reports and logs; reports and copies stay in gitignored `runs/` and `watch/`; URL fetching waits for the SSRF controls of design M6.3.
5. **Phase A1 now.** Contracts, MD/HTML ingest and heading segmenter, C2 published profile, article diff and legal-ref mapping, Mode A and B runners with a mock LLM, registry, `scripts/monitor.ts`. Live tuning, DOCX/PDF, the published-policy auditor prompt and the eval gates follow in A2.
6. **Open KB work.** Legal-ref map for the Network Act, ARTC, ECA and the safety-measures notice; law version and article snapshots; heading patterns. The legal-ref map needs privacy-domain-expert review.
