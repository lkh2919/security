## Ruling

PM ruling on 2026-10-02. The council was architect (`opus`), privacy-domain-expert (`opus`), security-expert (`sonnet`), auditor (`sonnet`), kb-curator (`sonnet`) and docs-writer (`sonnet`). User decisions are marked (user).

1. **Peer Watch (user idea).** Each Lotte domain group holds Lotte affiliates, which are checked, and about five peers, which are reference only. Peer changes become P0, P1 and P2 signals, worded "업계 동향(참고) — 법적 요구사항 아님". A signal may raise priority, never severity. Peers are never rated.
2. **Finance option ① (user).** 신용정보법, 전자금융거래법 and 금융소비자보호법 are added to the watch list. Their prefixes map to manual review only. L.POINT paragraphs that touch finance law are flagged "금융 법령 해당 – 수동 검토". There is no finance rule pack and no finance drafting in Phase 1.
3. **Contest fit (user).** The design addresses the judging criteria directly:
   - workflow harness (AI 활용 적합성, 30): design C3;
   - company-wide extensibility (전사 적용 가능성, 20): design C4;
   - measured business effect (업무 효과성, 30): design C8.
4. **Build order and cut line.** As in design C9. DOCX/PDF, notifications, the confirmed-tier UI, the URL service and extra peer groups are cut first.
5. **Human approvals.** As in design C10. Peer fetching goes live only after legal sign-off on the registry.
