## Ruling

PM triaged the user request (2026-09-29 interview plus the PM scope update of the same day).
The request is to build a multi-agent team whose product drafts a Korean privacy policy
and terms of service from a service form, an interview recording, and a branching
Interview Template, for the Lotte Innovate AX contest (Track A, deadline 2026-10-23).

- **Design Gate:** not exempt. The architect produced the design doc before any dispatch.
- **Spec:** new. Registered as `2026-09-29-privacy-policy-agent-team-design` with status `proposed` and source `architect`.
- **Placement:** option (a), `Projects/privacy-agent/`. The harness zone (`agents/`, `skills/`, `docs/`) follows workspace rules. The product zone (`packages/`, `kb/`, `golden/`) uses product gates: typecheck, tests, golden-set regression, and security scan.
- **Scope amendments (PM update, 2026-09-29):**
  - Korea only.
  - Terms of service are generated next to the privacy policy (KFTC standard terms, ARTC, E-Commerce Act).
  - The Lotte clause library with per-clause provenance and a Lotte house-style layer is the core differentiator. It is retrieval, not fine-tuning.
  - A branching Interview Template becomes a deliverable.
  - `kimlawtech/korean-privacy-terms` (Apache-2.0) is benchmarked for concepts only. No text is copied, and the project NOTICES records it.
- **Hiring:** privacy-domain-expert (High, `opus`) and kb-curator (Medium, `sonnet`). Both are project-scoped, not L0. No separate terms expert: privacy-domain-expert covers terms law.
- **Audit:** one rubric framework with a privacy profile, a terms profile, and cross-document checks. The runtime auditor is an isolated Opus call with a code-enforced input allowlist. The audit-fix loop is capped at 3 iterations per document, then escalates to the user.
- **Law facts:** law facts enter the KB only with a verified source and date. Reported claims (a 2026 PIPA amendment with a turnover-based fine, KFTC standard terms number) go on R6's first-run verification list.
